- The survey matrix spans roughly 14,443 submissions by 718 variables, with age centered near 30.4 years (IQR 23–35) and ordinal demographic markers concentrated in a few categories (e.g., `gendered` ≈13,537 vs. 906, ethnicity dominated by “Caucasian/white,” and country code dominated by the U.S./U.K./Canada).
