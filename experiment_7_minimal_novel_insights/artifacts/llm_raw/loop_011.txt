**Literature & Data**
- The survey has 14,443 respondents and 718 items capturing demographics plus detailed childhood socialization (e.g., “during ages 0-12/13-18: taught purity culture...”) alongside adult monogamy preferences, romantic satisfaction, and mental‑health markers, so there’s ample variation to test novel pathways.
- Both purity‑culture indicators show wide spreads (e.g., ~4,900 respondents strongly disagreeing and ~3,500 strongly agreeing for the age‑0‑12 item) and the adult monogamy scale has four distinct poles (full/leaning mono vs. nonmono), so statistical power should be sufficient.
- Sawatsky et al. (2024, *Sociology of Religion*, DOI 10.1093/socrel/srae031) documents that evangelical purity tropes heighten sexual pain and reduce marital/sexual satisfaction, highlighting how purity messaging enforces heteronormative monogamy.
- Evans et al. (2020, *American Journal of Sexuality Education*, DOI 10.1080/15546128.2020.1763883) shows that abstinence‑only versus comprehensive sex ed predicts later sexual contentment and communication satisfaction, implying early messaging leaves lasting marks on adult relationship well‑being.

**Hypotheses & Modeling**

- Hypothesis 2: Those same childhood purity cues will correlate with lower romantic satisfaction/self‑love and more mental‑health strain in adulthood, mirroring Evans et al.’s link between abstinence messaging and diminished sexual satisfaction but generalizing it beyond young adults to broader psychological well‑being.
- I’ll treat the purity measures as ordered exposures (separate models for each developmental window) and model monogamy preference with ordinal regression while probing satisfaction via OLS/logistic models, always accounting for current religion, class, and demographics so the effect isn’t just residual conservative identity.
- A follow‑up analysis will explore whether mental‑health indicators (e.g., depression/anxiety items) mediate the purity culture → satisfaction pathway, which keeps the study from being a mere replication of prior findings.
