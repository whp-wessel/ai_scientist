- **Parental support intensifies purity penalties.** The registered purity×parent-support interactions remain negative even though parental support itself boosts self-love (+0.212, p<10⁻³³) and romantic satisfaction (+0.190, p<10⁻¹⁸), so high-support families end up with a steeper purity decline in self-love (β=−0.0404, p=0.0054, Cohen’s d≈−0.022) and in romantic satisfaction (β=−0.0376, p=0.037, d≈−0.017) while anxiety stays flat, contradicting the buffering story that guided the pre-registered hypotheses built on Klement (2022) and Franck & Buehler (2007) and summarized in the introduction (`papers/purity_support_paper.tex:100`). See `tables/regression_results.csv:4`, `tables/regression_results.csv:3`, `tables/regression_results.csv:16`, and `tables/regression_results.csv:15` for the coefficients and significance.

- **Gender-minority identity does not worsen purity penalties.** The purity×gender-minority slope on self-love is small and non-significant (β≈0.079, p=0.195), yet gender minorities start lower in self-love (β≈−0.314, p<10⁻⁷) and higher in anxiety (β≈0.121, p=0.010); the only robust gender-minority interaction is that parental support generates smaller romantic-satisfaction gains (β≈−0.220, p=0.0019), so the counterintuitive steepening applies across identities rather than being driven by minority stress (`tables/regression_results_parent_support_gender_minority.csv:6`, `tables/regression_results_parent_support_gender_minority.csv:4`, and `tables/regression_results_parent_support_gender_minority.csv:20`).

- **Distancing via nonmonogamy erases the self-love penalty.** Preferring consensual nonmonogamy predicts +0.226 self-love and +0.225 romantic-satisfaction points (both p<10⁻⁶) while the purity slope for self-love flips from −0.053 among monogamous respondents to +0.012 among nonmonogamous respondents, so script resistance wipes out the purity penalty for self-love even though romantic satisfaction still declines with purity (`tables/regression_results_monogamy.csv:2`, `tables/regression_results_monogamy.csv:3`, `tables/regression_results_monogamy.csv:4`, `tables/regression_results_monogamy.csv:6`, `tables/regression_results_monogamy.csv:7`, and `tables/simple_slopes_monogamy.json:2-35`).
