Final release: the LaTeX manuscript now tells the full story (abstract line 96) about the `childhoodbalancedpublic_original.csv` cohort (n=14,400, 8.3% gender-minority, ω≈0.56, per the Methods at `papers/purity_support_paper.tex:103`) while the frozen pre-analysis plan (`analysis/pre_analysis_plan.md:3`) anchors the registered hypotheses. Figure 1 (`papers/purity_support_paper.tex:130`) visualizes the counterintuitive purity × parental-support trajectories that the paper uses to argue for more nuanced parenting dynamics.

- **Purity × support effects:** HC3-robust models show that a 1-SD increase in adolescent purity messaging erodes self-love (β≈−0.040, `tables/regression_results.csv:2`) and romantic satisfaction (β≈−0.111, `tables/regression_results.csv:14`), even as parental support boosts both outcomes (β≈0.212, `tables/regression_results.csv:3`; β≈0.190, `tables/regression_results.csv:15`). The purity × support interaction stays negative with confidence intervals that exclude zero for self-love (β≈−0.040, `tables/regression_results.csv:4`) and romantic satisfaction (β≈−0.038, `tables/regression_results.csv:16`), yielding the marginal slopes presented in the figure (`papers/purity_support_paper.tex:130`) and matching the simple-slope table (`tables/simple_slopes.json`).

- **Group-specific dynamics:** Gender-minority respondents report substantially lower self-love (β≈−0.338, `tables/regression_results.csv:40`), but purity messaging does not exacerbate that gap (purity×gender-minority β≈0.067, `tables/regression_results.csv:41`, ns). Preference for consensual nonmonogamy predicts about +0.226 self-love and +0.225 romantic satisfaction points (`tables/regression_results_monogamy.csv:3` & `:7`), and the purity penalty for self-love disappears once the nonmonogamy interaction (β≈0.066, `tables/regression_results_monogamy.csv:4`) offsets the base −0.053 slope, even though the romantic-satisfaction slope remains negative (`tables/regression_results_monogamy.csv:6-8`).

- **Reproducibility & documentation:** The reproducibility section (`papers/purity_support_paper.tex:220`) points to `analysis/analysis_pipeline.py`, `analysis/sensitivity_analysis.py`, and the derived tables/figures, while `outputs/sample_summary.json:2-4` and `:21` spell out the analytic sample and parent-support reliability. The marginal data for Figure 1 are archived at `analysis/marginal_self_love_data.csv` as noted at `papers/purity_support_paper.tex:221`, and all model code remains tied to the frozen plan.

- **Limitations & open questions:** The paper explicitly flags the cross-sectional retrospection (`papers/purity_support_paper.tex:214`), the modest parent-support composite reliability (`papers/purity_support_paper.tex:215`), missing doctrinal specificity (`papers/purity_support_paper.tex:216`), and unresolved gender-minority heterogeneity across identity/religiosity/race contexts (`papers/purity_support_paper.tex:217`) as avenues for future scrutiny.
