**Analysis Update**
- **Sample & reliability.** The analytic sample remains at 14,400 complete cases with 1,196 gender-minority respondents (≈8.3% of the sample) and the parent-support composite shows only moderate consistency (α-ish ≈0.56), so we flag the moderator’s limited reliability while reporting the core descriptives (`outputs/sample_summary.json:2`, `outputs/sample_summary.json:4`, `outputs/sample_summary.json:17`).
- **Purity × parental support (Hyp1).** Parental support has a strong positive main effect on adult self-love, yet the pre-registered purity13×support interaction is significantly negative for self-love and romantic satisfaction (coefs −0.040 and −0.038, p=0.005 and p=0.037) with Cohen’s d ≈−0.022, meaning the predicted purity penalty is negligible at low support but surfaces only when support is high—counter to the buffering hypothesis (`tables/regression_results.csv:3`, `tables/regression_results.csv:4`, `tables/regression_results.csv:16`). Simple slopes show that at −1 SD of support purity hardly moves self-love (slope ≈0.0003) while at +1 SD the penalty is about −0.0805 (see `tables/simple_slopes.json:7`, `tables/simple_slopes.json:11`).
- **Purity × gender-minority stress (Hyp2).** The purity13×gender-minority interaction is small and statistically indistinguishable from zero (≈0.067, p≈0.25) and the simple slopes for self-love, romantic satisfaction, and anxiety show only tiny differences—0.067 (SE 0.059), 0.068 (SE 0.072), and −0.055 (SE 0.045), respectively—so the data do not support a systematically larger purity penalty for gender-minority adults (`tables/regression_results.csv:41`, `tables/simple_slopes.json:40`, `tables/simple_slopes.json:76`, `tables/simple_slopes.json:112`).
- **Reproducibility artifacts.** Rerunning `analysis/analysis_pipeline.py` (starting around `analysis/analysis_pipeline.py:292`) regenerates the regression table, sample summary, and the parental-support margin plot (via `analysis/analysis_pipeline.py:319`), while `analysis/simple_slope_summary.py:93` produces the simple-slope JSON; the margin visualization is saved as `figures/marginal_self_love.png`, so every numerical and visual deliverable from this phase can be rebuilt from the scripts.
