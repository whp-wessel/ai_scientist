- **Release Summary**
- The pre-registered Parental-support × adolescent-purity interaction is significantly negative for self-love (β = −0.040, p = 0.005, Cohen’s d ≈ −0.021, n = 14,400) and for romantic satisfaction (β = −0.0376, p = 0.037), so higher support sharpens rather than buffers the purity penalty in the main models (`tables/regression_results.csv:4`, `tables/regression_results.csv:16`).
- Robustness slices keep the same counterintuitive pattern: the guidance component drives the interaction (β = −0.055, p = 0.0002), controls for unconditional-love memories and a trimmed sample keep β ≈ −0.04, and the purity×support×gender-minority term stays null yet documents a smaller romantic-satisfaction payoff for gender-minority respondents, so the tables archive every registered sensitivity story (`tables/regression_results_parent_support_components.csv:4`, `tables/regression_results_with_unconditional_love_controls.csv:4`, `tables/regression_results_trimmed_purity_support.csv:4`, `tables/regression_results_parent_support_gender_minority.csv:6`).
- Among currently religious respondents the interaction shrinks (β = −0.0299, p = 0.229), but current religiosity amplifies the early-childhood (0–12) purity penalty on self-love (β = −0.109, p < 0.001) and mirrors that penalty on anxiety (β = −0.103, p = 0.003), so the earliest window remains most sensitive even as the adolescence window stays null (`tables/regression_results_current_religion.csv:4`, `tables/regression_results_religion_interactions.csv:6`, `tables/regression_results_religion_interactions.csv:13`).

- **Limitations & Open Questions**
- The parent-support composite keeps modest internal consistency (ω ≈ 0.56), so separating guidance and humor or gathering triangulated family-context data would help interpret the moderation more confidently (`analysis/analysis_notes.md:136`).
- The negative moderation flips the pre-registered buffering story, hinting that highly involved parents may have doubled down on purity norms; that counterintuitive pattern deserves qualitative follow-up before causal claims (`analysis/analysis_notes.md:137`).
- Gender-minority moderation remains null despite large main disadvantages, leaving open whether specific purity doctrines or intersectional statuses could elicit different interactions once more granular data are available (`analysis/analysis_notes.md:138`).
